SCRIPT RULES (use in Stage 5)
==================================================
Long-form structure:
1. Hook (0:00–0:30): a verified shock number or paradox, then the core question. Never say "in this video".
2. Stakes and promise (to about 1:15).
3. Context (only the history needed).
4. Cause 1 (claim → number → visual → consequence; one idea per 60–90 seconds).
5. Re-hook around 40% in ("But that's not the real problem…").
6. Causes 2–3, escalating to the most counter-intuitive.
7. The reveal (who benefits / why it's hard to fix / the surprise winner).
8. What's next (best, base and worst scenarios, labelled as forecasts).
9. Close (answer the title's question directly; one honest open question; end within 20 seconds).

Shorts (30–60s) use: hook → one mechanism → reveal → open question.

Explaining technique: the ladder method.
1. Everyday analogy.
2. The real term.
3. One number.
4. One comparison ("that's €4,000 per citizen").
5. One consequence.

Further script rules:
- At most one new technical term per minute, explained immediately, always using the same word for the same thing.
- Signpost and recap in one sentence every 4–5 minutes.
- Open loops ("we'll come back to why that number matters"), list promises ("three reasons; the third explains everything"), and escalation.
- About 20–25 spoken words per 10-second clip (about 145 words per minute), fewer for dramatic pauses.
- Use an original narrator voice: calm, confident, curious, never an imitation of a real person.
