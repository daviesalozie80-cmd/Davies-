# Niche Demand & Competition: Ancient Humans / Prehistory / Anthropology / Evolutionary Psychology Channels (YouTube/TikTok)

Research date: 2026-11-16. Method note: WebFetch could not reach most hosts from this session (falconvid.ai, vidiq.com, youtube.com, threads.com, medium.com, blog.autonolab.com all failed DNS, so those hosts would need to be allowed in network settings). Every figure below comes from search-result snippets and summaries of the cited pages, not from full page reads. Most subscriber counts come from third-party trackers or marketing blogs and conflict across snapshots. Treat them as approximate.

## Which channels operate in this niche, and how big and fast-growing are they?

### Takeaway
The niche has a few large personality-led anthropology and archaeology channels (Miniminuteman ~3.3M, Stefan Milo ~650K, StoneAgeMan ~500K, plus Primitive Technology at 11M+ as a skills-adjacent giant). Below them is a fast-growing layer of faceless, often AI-assisted "ancient humans" channels, many from 2025–2026, in the roughly 10K–170K range. Sleep-history channels (Sleepless Historian ~716K) are a related, crowded format.

### Cited Findings
**Established and personality-led (not faceless)**
- Primitive Technology: over 11M subscribers and over 1.24B views as of Nov 2026. The creator stayed anonymous for the channel's first two years. It is wordless primitive-skills content, not narrated. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primitive_Technology)
- Miniminuteman (Milo Rossi, archaeology science communicator): about 3.36M YouTube subscribers (data dated June 18, 2026), about 3.3–3.4M on trackers in mid-2026, and about 6.2M across Instagram, YouTube and TikTok. — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/miniminuteman/); [Favikon](https://app.favikon.com/public/profile/milo-rossi-6a2a4892b6727dd919415773/)
- Stefan Milo (human evolution and prehistory explainers): estimates run from about 564K (vidIQ, Aug 2025 data) to about 647K (SponsorRadar) and 654K (OutlierKit). — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCZ9jWH_8tJ-Nmaj8dSQdEYA); [SponsorRadar](https://sponsorradar.com/channels/stefanmilo); [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/channel/stefanmilo)
- Ancient Americas: about 303K subscribers, listed by SponsorRadar as a similar channel (undated). — [SponsorRadar](https://sponsorradar.com/channels/stefanmilo)
- StoneAgeMan (Rob Nelson; science, history, human biology, nature): about 503K subscribers per PLAYBOARD (snapshot date unknown). — [PLAYBOARD](https://playboard.co/en/channel/UCFAbxaVl6PJMwbMMXX9ZcNw)

**Faceless and "ancient humans" explainer layer (mostly 2025–2026)**
- Mack (@MackExplains7, stickman explainer; "Secret Lives of Ancient Humans" series): cached YouTube page counts of about 88.2K and 90.4K. A 2026 roundup says the channel was created in May 2026 and reached 89.5K. A blog says the series took a five-month-old channel to almost 90K. OutlierKit's older snapshot shows 74.5K subscribers and 9.0M total views. — [YouTube channel page](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCaS30yIIV6y2zqSnHGdIGMQ); [OutlierKit – Mack](https://outlierkit.com/channel/mackexplains7); [TaleTok roundup](https://taletok.io/blog/best-faceless-youtube-channels/); [FalconVid](https://falconvid.ai/blog/history-youtube-channels-that-make-money)
- Mack also sells a Teachable course called "Secret Lives of Ancient Humans", which cites "50k+ subscribers, 6M+ views" (older figure). This shows the creator is monetizing through education products as well as ads. — [Teachable course page](https://ancient-minds-academy1.teachable.com/p/the-secret-lives-of-ancient-humans)
- Axen (stickman explainer, same ancient-humans niche): the roundup says it was created in April 2026 and reached 63.9K subscribers. FalconVid reports 24 videos, 6.4M views and about 266K views per upload. OutlierKit's older snapshot shows 47.2K subscribers and 4.9M views. — [TaleTok](https://taletok.io/blog/best-faceless-youtube-channels/); [OutlierKit – Axen](https://outlierkit.com/channel/axencool); [FalconVid](https://falconvid.ai/blog/history-youtube-channels-that-make-money)

- Prehistoric Humans-100s: describes itself as AI-generated content showing prehistoric life, survival, hunting and interactions with animals, with about 170K subscribers on the page snapshot. — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC-624j5930miQvG21Z4VsIw)
- Ancient Bloodline: "day in the life" documentary films reconstructing how early humans lived, "based on experimental archaeology and anthropology". It says it plans a new film every day and shows about 99.3K subscribers in page metadata. — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCEaFfr7EmmFALok_GjDeErg)

**Sleep-history and second-person "you" formats**
- Sleepless Historian: 716K subscribers per vidIQ as of 2026-10-29, but it gained only about 1K subscribers between Oct 1 and Oct 28, 2026, so growth has plateaued. Its top video is "Boring History For Sleep" about medieval peasants, with about 2.54M views. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@sleeplesshistorian/)
- A TubeLab post from about mid-2025 said Sleepless Historian had about 150K subscribers and 3.5M+ views in one month. It credited the "Why You Wouldn't Last a Day as X" trend applied to bedtime history ("Bedtime stories are saturated? Bring the 'Why You Wouldn't Last a Day as X' trend to it"). — [Threads / TubeLab](https://www.threads.com/@tubelabhq/post/DJWyYWWOzKw/the-sleepless-historian-is-a-great-example-of-bringing-trends-into-a-boring-nich)
- Chat History (animated, second-person "Why You Wouldn't Survive" series, founded July 11, 2022): a recurring character keeps dying across historical eras, and "Why You Wouldn't Survive in Ancient Egypt" has about 3.6M views. — [List of Deaths wiki](https://listofdeaths.fandom.com/wiki/Chat_History's_%22Why_You_Wouldn't_Survive%22_Series); [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/WebVideo/ChatHistory)
- The "Why You Wouldn't Survive" title format is widely copied. Examples include Historian Sleepy (1.5–3 hour videos, top video "Why You Wouldn't Survive a Day in the Wild West" at about 620K views), a Drowseworthy playlist, and videos on Hadrian's Wall and Han Dynasty topics. — [Autonolab](https://blog.autonolab.com/niches/2025-12-10-faceless-youtube-history/); [Drowseworthy playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLWP13xAvGR-6-1axHURlvd0x_9rG29tlm); [Hadrian's Wall video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vKuYV8hD1r4)

### Inferences
- The faceless ancient-humans space looks like a 2025–2026 gold rush. Many channels have generic names ("Ancient X", "Million Years Ago") and AI visuals, which points to a low barrier to entry and many copycats.
- The largest channels (Miniminuteman, Stefan Milo) are built on credibility and personality. Faceless channels win on packaging ("Why did ancient humans...", stickman visuals) and upload volume.

### Gaps
- Social Blade growth curves for these channels could not be fetched (host access). Start dates for Ancient Bloodline, Prehistoric Humans-100s and Genesis Chamber are unknown.
- Snapshots conflict (for example Mack at 74.5K vs 90.4K, Axen at 47.2K vs 63.9K). These are probably timing differences, but the dates are unconfirmed.
- I found no data on TikTok-native ancient-humans account sizes.
- I found no specific evolutionary-psychology explainer channels with subscriber data.

## Have any new history/anthropology/"ancient humans" channels reached 10K–100K+ subscribers within about 3 months in 2024–2026?

### Takeaway
Yes, several reportedly have, mostly in 2026 and mostly per marketing blogs. Mack (created about May 2026) reached about 75–90K within roughly 3–6 months. Axen (created about April 2026) reached about 47–64K. An unnamed AI caveman channel reportedly hit 60M views at about 3 months old. None of this is independently verified.

### Cited Findings
- Mack and Axen, "two stickman explainer channels created in spring 2026", are said to be at 89.5K and 63.9K subscribers and to have "passed 60K subscribers within months". — [TaleTok](https://taletok.io/blog/best-faceless-youtube-channels/)
- "Secret Lives of Ancient Humans" videos reportedly took a five-month-old channel (Mack) to almost 90K subscribers. — [FalconVid](https://falconvid.ai/blog/history-youtube-channels-that-make-money)
- Axen: 24 videos, 6.4M views, about 266K views per upload. A small catalogue drives high per-video averages. — [FalconVid](https://falconvid.ai/blog/history-youtube-channels-that-make-money)
- A Medium how-to post (about Oct 2025, roughly 395 days old) describes a roughly 3-month-old AI channel with about 60M views, one video at 40M views in a month, and a top video of a prehistoric human attacked by a snake. The author says it was made with AI thumbnails and Google's video model (Veo). The figures are self-reported and the channel is not named in the snippet. — [Medium](https://medium.com/write-a-catalyst/this-3-month-old-ai-channel-got-60-million-views-and-how-anyone-can-do-it-f0c332cfaf81)
- AI "Caveman vs Lion" and "Caveman vs Giant Snake" style videos exist as a genre on YouTube (no view counts retrieved). — [Caveman vs Lion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMZrO3Uabcg); [Caveman vs Giant Snake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Llt7mxxrxBY)
- Sleepless Historian reportedly reached about 150K subscribers and 3.5M+ monthly views by about May 2025 using the "Why You Wouldn't Last a Day" spin on sleep history. Its start date is not confirmed. — [Threads / TubeLab](https://www.threads.com/@tubelabhq/post/DJWyYWWOzKw/the-sleepless-historian-is-a-great-example-of-bringing-trends-into-a-boring-nich)
- Baseline for comparison: one (non-ancient-history) creator took 787 days, from Apr 9, 2023 to Jun 4, 2025, to reach 100K. Fast growth is atypical. — [Very Normal Substack](https://verynormal.substack.com/p/issue-41-a-lifelong-goal-achieved)

### Inferences
- "Ancient humans + stickman/AI explainer + question title" is the specific sub-format with documented 2026 breakouts. Fast growth seems to come from a few high-performing videos (about 266K average per upload for Axen) rather than volume.
- Survivorship bias is strong. These examples come from blogs that sell faceless-video tools (FalconVid, TaleTok), which have an incentive to showcase outliers.

### Gaps
- I could not verify exact creation dates or 3-month subscriber snapshots via Social Blade.
- I found no Reddit r/NewTubers case study specifically about an ancient-humans channel (search returned nothing relevant).

## What search and trend demand exists?

### Takeaway
Hard search-volume data was not found. Demand evidence is indirect: multi-million-view videos, the long-running TikTok "ArchaeologyTok"/Ancestors trend, "caveman" lifestyle trends, and large audiences for archaeology creators.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok's "Ancestors" / "History Repeats Itself" trend was started in 2023 by ArchaeologyTok creator @archthot with a Neanderthals-eating-crabs video, and "became a regular fixture" on the app. — [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-news/tiktok-ancestor-trend-history-repeats-itself-1234686063/)
- "Caveman skincare" (a zero-product routine framed around hunter-gatherers) trended on TikTok around early 2025. — [Revivalabs](https://www.revivalabs.com/caveman-skincare-why-tiktok-is-falling-for-a-bare-bones-routine/)
- TikTok has active discovery pages for hunter-gatherer facial reconstructions (e.g. Ancestral Whispers, an 8,500-year-old Karelia man) and persistence-hunting explainers. — [TikTok discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/what-is-a-hunter-gatherer-skull?lang=en); [TikTok discover – ancestors hunting](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/our-ancestors-used-to-hunt-those-prey)
- Video-level demand signals: Sleepless Historian's medieval peasants sleep video at about 2.54M views, Chat History's Ancient Egypt video at about 3.6M, and Axen's about 266K average per upload. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@sleeplesshistorian/); [List of Deaths wiki](https://listofdeaths.fandom.com/wiki/Chat_History's_%22Why_You_Wouldn't_Survive%22_Series); [FalconVid](https://falconvid.ai/blog/history-youtube-channels-that-make-money)
- Google Trends Explore supports a YouTube-search property filter, but it adds privacy noise to low-volume terms. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-trends/)
- A viral AI caveman meme story (China, Nov 16, 2026) shows caveman imagery still circulates as a meme format. — [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/11/16/china-influencer-slammed-for-using-ai-to-resurrect-grandpa-on-hit-fashion-account-to-grieve)

### Inferences
- Evergreen curiosity about how ancient humans lived appears steady and well-served by recommendations rather than search. Discovery is driven by browse and suggested feeds and by title curiosity gaps.

## How saturated is the niche, and what angles are underserved?

### Takeaway
The generic faceless "ancient humans / caveman survival" format, especially with AI visuals, is getting crowded and faces policy risk. Credible, research-backed second-person narration that ties prehistory to modern life (work, sleep, diet, relationships, evolutionary psychology) appears less crowded, but this is an inference rather than measured.

### Cited Findings
- Many lookalike channels exist: Ancient Bloodline, Ancient Tribe Stories, 2 Million Years Ago, Million Years Ago (two separate handles), Prehistoric Humans-100s, Primitive Past, Ancient Human Files, Hominid History Hub, Mack, Axen and Stickly. — see channel links above; [Million Years Ago](https://www.youtube.com/@millionyearsago168); [Million Years Ago (2)](https://www.youtube.com/@MillionYearsAgo-i7b)
- Sleep-history is described as saturated. Sleepless Historian's edge was importing the "Why You Wouldn't Last a Day" trend. — [Threads / TubeLab](https://www.threads.com/@tubelabhq/post/DJWyYWWOzKw/the-sleepless-historian-is-a-great-example-of-bringing-trends-into-a-boring-nich)
- "A $20 CPM means nothing if 200 established channels already own every keyword." — [OutlierKit/other niche guide via search](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-niches-guide/)
- Policy risk: from July 15, 2025, YouTube's renamed "inauthentic content" policy (formerly "repetitious content") targets mass-produced and repetitive material. YouTube says it is not a ban on faceless channels, but channels without a distinct human voice or creative direction face more scrutiny. Penalties range from video demonetization to YPP removal. — [Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/07/08/youtube-targets-inauthentic-content/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/youtube-updates-monetisation-policies-ai-and-repetitive-content-ban-begins-july-15-1.500192660); [Fliki](https://fliki.ai/blog/youtube-monetization-policy-2025)
- Ancient Bloodline explicitly positions itself on "experimental archaeology and anthropology", so research-backed framing is already used as a differentiator by at least one competitor. — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCEaFfr7EmmFALok_GjDeErg)
- Mack's "Were Some Ancient Humans Vegan?" and "What Were Relationships Like in Prehistoric Times?" show question-led, modern-relevance topics already being used. — [Video 1](https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=glwkUodkixQ); [Video 2](https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=vKxymlu_6VU)

### Inferences
- Angles that look relatively underserved, from the gaps observed rather than measured:
  1. Research-backed and cited evolutionary-psychology explainers ("why your brain hates open-plan offices / Monday meetings, explained by hunter-gatherers"). I found no dedicated faceless evo-psych channel with notable scale.
  2. Second-person "you are a hunter-gatherer" narration tied to modern work and life. Second-person survival exists for historical eras (Chat History, sleep-history), but less so for deep prehistory with a modern-life payoff.
  3. Credible alternatives to AI "caveman vs lion" content. Fact-checked, original-voice content is also the safest position under the inauthentic-content policy.
- The fastest-growing competitors are recent stickman/AI channels, so a new entrant competes on title and thumbnail packaging against them, and on credibility against Stefan Milo and Miniminuteman.

### Gaps
- I found no quantitative saturation metric (e.g. number of channels or uploads per week in the niche).
- There are no data on audience overlap or on whether viewers prefer research-backed or dramatized content.

## What are typical RPM/CPM rates for history and education content?

### Takeaway
Third-party estimates put long-form history RPM at roughly $6–15 (CPM around $6–12+). Education CPM is around $9–15. History Shorts earn only about $0.04–0.25 RPM. Dark history is lower ($3–7 CPM). Sleep content is long-form, which allows more mid-roll ads. All of these are blog estimates, not YouTube data.

### Cited Findings
- History RPM is $6–14 ("History YouTube Channel in 2026: $6–14 RPM"), placing it in the middle tier behind finance. — [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/blog/history-youtube-channel-guide-2026)
- History CPM is $6–12. — [OutlierKit niches guide](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-niches-guide/)
- History automation RPM is $8–15. — [Fluxnote automation niches](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-automation-niches-2026)
- Education CPM is $9–15, general knowledge $8–15, and finance education $20–50. — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/blog/most-profitable-youtube-niches); [Virlo](https://virlo.ai/blog/highest-rpm-niches-on-youtube)
- History Shorts RPM is about $0.04–0.10 (Fluxnote 2026) or a $0.10–0.25 planning range (Satura, 2025). — [Fluxnote Shorts](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-rpm-history-niche); [Satura](https://saturaai.com/history-military-youtube-shorts-rpm)
- Dark history CPM is $3–7. — [Fluxnote dark history](https://fluxnote.io/guides/faceless-dark-history-youtube-channel-guide)
- "History and education is a legitimate niche but not the highest-RPM category." — [Autonolab](https://blog.autonolab.com/niches/2025-12-10-faceless-youtube-history/)
- FalconVid headlines faceless history channels as earning up to "$218K/month by 2026". This is a marketing claim and should not be relied on. — [FalconVid](https://falconvid.ai/blog/history-youtube-channels-that-make-money)

### Inferences
- For a channel with 1M monthly long-form views, the ranges above imply roughly $6K–15K per month in ad revenue (my arithmetic). TikTok and Shorts views monetize poorly, so they mainly serve as discovery funnels.
- Mack's Teachable course shows that additional income from products or courses is a viable add-on in this niche.

### Gaps
- There is no first-party RPM data from a creator in the prehistory or anthropology niche specifically.
- The RPM sources are all creator-tool vendor blogs and the estimates conflict.
