## YouTube "About" description (draft)
Every place on Earth has a story someone tried to bury.

The Hidden Map is a documentary channel about real conspiracies, cover-ups and unexplained events — from Africa to Europe, Asia to the Americas and Oceania. Each episode drops a pin on one location and asks: what really happened here?

We follow the paper trail — court records, declassified files, investigations and eyewitness accounts — and separate what's proven from what's still rumour.

New documentary every week.
Sources for every episode are in the description.

## Visual identity
- **Logo:** a folded paper map with one red pin; the pin's shadow forms a keyhole.
- **Colours:** dark navy/charcoal background, aged-paper beige for maps, one accent red for pins and highlights.
- **Fonts:** a condensed bold sans-serif for titles (e.g. Bebas Neue, Oswald) + a typewriter font for "case file" details (e.g. Special Elite).
- **Banner:** a dark world map with six faint red pins (one per continent) and the tagline "Every place has a secret."
- **Episode intro (2–3 s):** zoom from the globe into the location, the red pin drops, the location name types out.
- **Thumbnail system:** location map crop + red pin + one striking real image + max 4 words of text. No gore.

## Recurring series ideas
- **Pins on the Map** — main weekly documentary, one location per episode.
- **One Per Continent** — a 6-part season, one real cover-up from each continent.
- **Proven or Myth?** — shorter episodes testing a famous conspiracy against the evidence.
