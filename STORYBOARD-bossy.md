# Storyboard — "What Did Ancient Humans Do to Someone Bossy?"

Script: `scripts/bossy-script.txt` · Style: `STYLE-GUIDE.md` · References: `assets/reference/`

**How to use:** each scene is one shot. **VO** is the narration line. **Visual** is what's on screen.
**Prompt** goes after the prompt prefix in `STYLE-GUIDE.md` §8. **Text** is added in the editor, not
generated. **Reuse** marks frames where a reference image already works, either as-is or with a small edit.
**Motion** is the animation for the shot.

Recurring cast (generate these once, then reuse them for consistency):
- **YOU:** the base stick figure (`10-base-character.webp`) in a fur tunic, holding a spear.
- **THE CAMP:** a ring of small faceless brown stick figures around the campfire.
- **RICHARD LEE:** the anthropologist from `07-ox-anthropologist.jpg` (pith hat, glasses, khaki shirt).
- **MODERN YOU:** the base figure in a plain shirt holding a phone (`05-today-city-walk.jpg`).

## ACT 1 — The hook

**1.** VO: "You walk back into camp with fresh meat on your shoulder, and start telling everyone what to do."
Visual: YOU walk in, chest puffed, a big leg of meat on one shoulder, the campfire behind.
Prompt: `stick figure caveman in fur tunic, smug expression, carrying a large leg of meat over his shoulder, walking toward a small campfire with stone huts, daytime sky #CDECFE`
Motion: slow push-in. The character bobs as he walks.

**2.** VO: "Where to sit. Who eats first. Who hunts tomorrow."
Visual: YOU point; three small icons pop in one after another (a log seat, a bowl, a spear pointing to the horizon).
Prompt: `stick figure caveman pointing bossily, three simple icons floating beside him: a log seat, a wooden food bowl, a spear`
Text: `WHERE TO SIT` / `WHO EATS FIRST` / `WHO HUNTS` (one per beat).
Motion: icons pop in on each phrase.

**3.** VO: "No manager. No HR. No written rules."
Visual: three modern icons (a tie, an HR badge, a rulebook), each crossed out with a red X.
Prompt: `three simple icons in a row on cream paper: a necktie, an ID badge, a closed rulebook, flat cartoon`
Text: red X draws over each one.

**4.** VO: "Most people picture the strongest hunter as the chief."
Visual: a muscular caveman wearing a crown made of bones, with a thought bubble over a crowd.
Prompt: `muscular stick figure caveman with big arms wearing a crude crown of bones, standing on a rock, proud`
Motion: zoom in on the crown.

**5.** VO: "The evidence says the opposite."
Visual: the same frame flips, and the crown falls off with a dust puff.
Text: `THE OPPOSITE`. Motion: whip-pan, then the crown drops.

**6.** VO: "So what did they do to you?"
Visual: YOU alone in the centre, worried, with a ring of camp eyes in the dark around him.
Prompt: `worried stick figure caveman in the centre, surrounded at the edges by many pairs of eyes in shadow, campfire glow`
Motion: slow zoom-in, eyes blink.

## ACT 2 — The Christmas ox

**7.** VO: "In the Kalahari, one anthropologist learned the answer the hard way. With a Christmas gift."
Visual: a map of Africa with a red dot on the Kalahari, then a small gift box pops up.

**8.** VO: "In the 1960s, anthropologist Richard Lee was living with the Ju/'hoansi people of the Kalahari Desert."
Visual: RICHARD LEE beside grass huts on sand.
**Reuse:** `07-ox-anthropologist.jpg`. Show the background huts first, before the ox appears.
Text: `1960s`.

**9.** VO: "He bought the biggest ox he could find for a Christmas feast. Huge. Heavy. Perfect."
Visual: the ox with a red bow and a proud RICHARD LEE.
**Reuse:** `07-ox-anthropologist.jpg` as-is.

Prompt: `anthropologist stick figure in pith hat and round glasses, smile fading, surrounded by speech bubbles, beside a big ox`

**11.** VO: "Too thin. A bag of bones."
Visual: an X-ray-style outline of the ox showing only bones (a comic exaggeration).
Prompt: `cartoon ox drawn as a skinny skeleton outline inside a paper bag, comic exaggeration`
Text: `"A BAG OF BONES"`.

**12.** VO: "Even after the ox was cooked, and the fat turned out to be thick, the jokes kept coming."
Visual: the ox roasting over the fire, thick fat dripping, and the camp still laughing.
Prompt: `large roast over a campfire with thick fat dripping, ring of small stick figures laughing around it`

**13.** VO: "Lee was hurt. Then a man explained it to him."
Visual: a sad Lee, and an older Ju/'hoan man sitting next to him, talking calmly.
Prompt: `sad anthropologist stick figure in pith hat sitting on a log, an older stick figure man in simple wrap sitting beside him, explaining calmly, campfire`

**14.** VO: "When someone brings in a lot of meat, he starts to think of himself as a chief…"

**15.** VO: "So the camp calls his meat worthless. To cool his heart. To keep him gentle."
Visual: a red, steaming heart icon turns cool blue as the steam stops.
Prompt: `simple cartoon heart icon, left half hot red with steam, right half calm dusty blue`

**16.** VO: "It wasn't rudeness… It was a system."
Visual: gears or a blueprint over the camp circle.

## ACT 3 — Two hunters

Prompt: `two stick figure hunters in fur tunics standing side by side, neutral, cream background`

**18.** VO: "The first one brings home meat three days in a row."

**Reuse:** pose and mood from `08-ragged-caveman.jpg`.

**20.** VO: "One of them gets teased at the fire. Which one? Take a guess."

Visual: a spotlight lands on hunter 1.
**Reuse:** `09-the-weird-one.jpg` composition (a row of figures, one in a spotlight).

**22.** VO: "The hunter with nothing still eats, from everyone else's share."

Prompt: `ring of stick figures passing small pieces of meat to a tired thin hunter in the middle, warm campfire`

**23.** VO: "The hunter with too much gets the jokes."
Visual: hunter 1 blushing, with laughing "HA HA" marks around him.

**24.** VO: "So he learns to arrive quietly, sit down, and say he saw something small. Maybe. If anyone wants to look."

**25.** VO: "Here's why. Big game is luck as much as skill."

**26.** VO: "A great hunter can still go weeks without a kill. Animals run. Herds move. The rain comes late."

**27.** VO: "So the meat you share today is the meat you eat next month, when your own arrows come home clean."
Visual: a timeline arrow from TODAY (giving meat) to NEXT MONTH (receiving meat).
**Reuse:** the arrow-and-timeline layout from `11-stopwatch-18min.jpg`.

**28.** VO: "Not just kindness. Insurance."
Visual: the campfire inside a shield icon.
Text: `INSURANCE`.

**29.** VO: "And a man who starts to see himself as a chief starts to share a little less."
Visual: a progress-style "sharing" bar shrinking.
**Reuse:** `13-progress-bar-99pct.jpg`. Animate the bar draining.

**30.** VO: "A little girl copies your proud walk around the fire, chest out, spear raised. The whole camp laughs. Even you."
Visual: a small stick girl mimicking YOU, with the camp laughing and YOU laughing too.
Prompt: `small stick figure girl with a little spear, puffing out her chest, mimicking a proud walk around a campfire, everyone laughing including a caveman`

## ACT 4 — Boehm's ladder

**31.** VO: "But a joke only works on someone who cares what others think. What happened to the ones who didn't care?"
Visual: a stern bully with arms crossed and jokes bouncing off him.
Prompt: `stern stick figure caveman with arms crossed, speech bubbles bouncing off him`

**32.** VO: "In 1993, anthropologist Christopher Boehm published a survey of 48 groups where nobody was officially in charge."
Visual: a world map with 48 red dots appearing.
**Reuse:** `15-map-world-routes.jpg`. Remove the labels and arcs, then add the dots.
Text: `1993` · `48 GROUPS`.

**33.** VO: "Different continents. Different languages. People who had never met."
Visual: speech bubbles in different scripts over the map.

**34.** VO: "And he kept finding the same ladder."
Visual: a tall wooden ladder with 4 rungs on cream.
Prompt: `simple wooden ladder with four rungs standing on cream paper, flat cartoon`

**35.** VO: "Step one. Laughter."  → rung 1 lights up. Icon: a laughing mouth. Text: `1. LAUGHTER`
**36.** VO: "Step two. Criticism. Out loud, in front of everyone." → rung 2. Icon: a pointing finger. Text: `2. CRITICISM`
**37.** VO: "Step three. Nobody listens. You give an order, and nobody moves." → rung 3. Icon: a muted speaker. Text: `3. NOBODY LISTENS`

**38.** VO: "Picture that step from the inside. You tell the young men to hunt east tomorrow."
Visual: YOU pointing right (EAST), with young hunters nodding.
Prompt: `bossy stick figure caveman pointing to the right, several young stick figure hunters nodding politely`

**39.** VO: "They nod. In the morning, every one of them has gone west. Nobody argued. Nobody had to."
Visual: dawn. YOU stand alone. Footprints lead off to the left (WEST).
Text: `EAST →` crossed out, `← WEST`. Motion: footprints appear one by one.

**40.** VO: "Step four. People leave. Or they make you leave." → rung 4. Icon: a door. Text: `4. LEAVE`

**41.** VO: "And at the very end, in rare cases, when one man became a real danger, the whole group acted together to remove him for good."
Visual: (keep it tasteful) a lone figure walking off into a dark horizon while the whole group stands together.
**Reuse:** mood from `08-ragged-caveman.jpg`, but in silhouette.

**42.** VO: "Boehm called it a reverse dominance hierarchy."
Visual: a pyramid of stick figures flipped upside down, with one boss at the bottom holding everyone up.
Text: `REVERSE DOMINANCE HIERARCHY`.

**43.** VO: "Not a group without power. A group where the followers held it."
Visual: many small hands holding a glowing campfire, or holding a leash on the boss.

**44.** VO: "One honest warning. These are recent societies, not Stone Age time capsules."
Visual: a time capsule with a red X, and a calendar showing modern years.
Text: `NOT A TIME CAPSULE`.

**45.** VO: "The Ju/'hoansi and the Hadza are modern people with their own histories."
Visual: the map with two dots, Kalahari and Tanzania.
**Reuse:** `15-map-world-routes.jpg` (it already shows TANZANIA and NAMIBIA).

**46.** VO: "But when the same pattern shows up again and again, in groups that never met, it starts to look very old."
Visual: the same ladder icon repeating across the world map.

**54.** VO: "After dark, the talk changed. Fewer complaints. More stories, about people in distant camps and relatives far away."

**56.** VO: "But it shows how a camp of a few dozen people could govern itself with nothing but words."

## ACT 8 — Northwest Coast

**75.** VO: "Which raises the real question. If this worked so well, why did it ever stop?"

Visual: a map of North America with a red dot on the Pacific Northwest.
**Reuse:** `15-map-world-routes.jpg`, zoomed into North America.

**77.** VO: "…they had huge salmon runs, and they could dry and store food through the winter, in big wooden houses."

**79.** VO: "Same way of getting food. Completely different politics."

**80.** VO: "…stored food changes the math. You can't easily walk away from a house full of winter salmon."

### Production checklist
1. Record the VO, or generate it with TTS, from `scripts/bossy-script.txt`.
2. Generate the recurring cast first, then each scene, using the prompt prefix.
3. Make the edits marked **Reuse** to the reference frames.
4. In the editor, put each frame on its VO line. Add the text, the motion from `STYLE-GUIDE.md` §7, and sound effects.
5. Export at 1920×1080, 30 fps. Keep the bottom 10% clear for subtitles.
